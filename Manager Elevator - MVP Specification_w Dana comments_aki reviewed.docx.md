## **WHAT THIS APP DOES** 

Manager Elevator is an AI-powered continuous improvement platform designed specifically for Black middle managers in corporate America—particularly HBCU graduates—who need practical, step-by-step guidance to lead their teams through successful continuous improvement (CI) events without expensive consultants or complex certifications. 

The platform combines a comprehensive masterclass with practical tracking tools and AI-powered coaching to help managers execute 14-week continuous improvement projects, document their results, and articulate their achievements for career advancement—all while building bulletproof  continuous improvement team culture. 

By transforming complex Lean Six Sigma theory into actionable weekly sessions with ready-to-use PowerPoints and tools, Manager Elevator enables middle managers to finally execute continuous improvement the right way, prove their value, and accelerate their careers. 

## **THE PROBLEM** 

Middle managers in corporate America—especially Black managers from HBCU backgrounds—are told to "do continuous improvement" but aren't given practical guidance on how to actually execute it. They're stuck between expensive consultants and theoretical certifications that don't translate to real-world application. 

**Key Pain Points** 

* **Lean Six Sigma doesn't teach execution:** Certifications provide theory but not step by-step implementation guidance   
* **Consultants are expensive and temporary:** Companies bring in consultants for events but don't build internal capability   
* **Service industries can't do 5-day kaizens:** Unlike manufacturing, service teams don't have redundant production lines (teams) that would allow a team to go offline to do an experiment sprint for a week without production falling seriously behind  
* **Can't articulate improvements:** Even managers who execute improvements struggle to document and communicate their value   
* **No career advancement toolkit:** Missing the ability to showcase continuous improvement success in performance reviews   
* **Lack of representation:** Black managers need culturally relevant support and community

  ## **CORE MVP FEATURES** 

  *These features are included in the MVP build:* 


  **1\. App Onboarding: "Ride the Manager Elevator to Career Success"** 

  Welcome experience that orients users to the platform, explains the journey ahead, and sets expectations for the 14-week continuous improvement process. Simple registration capturing name, email, company, industry, and current management level. 


  **2\. Leading Bulletproof Continuous Improvement Masterclass** 

  **The core educational content** delivered through Miestro (external integration). The masterclass is organized into 4 modules with 25 total lessons, covering the complete Continuous Improvement Done Right: 

  **Module 1: "The Value of Illuminating Workplace Problems" (7 Lessons)** 

* Understanding middle manager undervaluation   
* Introduction to CI Done Right Formula  
* Waste Warrior Exercises (recognizing, prioritizing, SIPOC, problem statements)  
* Maximizing next 30 days in current assignment 

  **Module 2: "Investigating Root Causes for Success" (7 Lessons)** 

* Waste WAR Battle 10 Steps introduction   
* Sessions 1-6 covering Steps 1-4 (Clarify, Define Metrics, Current State, Root Causes) 

  **Module 3: "Intelligently Testing Potential Solutions" (7 Lessons)** 

* Sessions 7-13 covering Steps 5-8 (Future State, Brainstorm, Test, Measure) 

  **Module 4: "Implementing Winning Solutions for Success" (4 Lessons)** 

* Session 14 covering Steps 9-10 (Implement, Share & Sustain)   
* Maximizing first 100 days in new assignments   
* Building team culture   
* Becoming a CI consultant 

  **Key Characteristics:** 

* Each lesson includes a PowerPoint deck for team sessions   
* Each lesson includes 1-3 downloadable tools (Excel, Word, templates)   
* 14 core sessions represent weekly 90-minute team meetings   
* Designed for service industries (not traditional 5-day manufacturing kaizens) 

  **3\. Continuous Improvement Done Right Trackers** 

  Three integrated tracking systems that help managers execute, document, and showcase their CI work: 


  **A. Improvement Opportunities Tracker** 

* Log and prioritize workplace waste and problems   
* Track which opportunities are active, completed, or deferred 

  **B. Waste WAR Battle Project Tracker** 

* Track progress through all 14 weekly sessions   
* Upload link to PDF Files of completed PowerPoints for each session   
* Link to external files to manage storage constraints   
* Notification system alerts Dana when users complete sessions 

  **C. Winning Solutions Impact Tracker** 

* Document measurable results from implementations   
* Calculate ROI and efficiency gains 

  **D. Mining for CI Success Nuggets** 

* Extract key achievements for performance reviews via ChatGPT or copilot  
* Build portfolio of documented improvements   
* Generate talking points for advancement conversations via ChatGPT or Copilot

  **4\. CI Done Right Success Dashboard** 

  Visual progress tracking showing the user's journey through the platform with badge/milestone system: 

  **Progress Visualization:** 

* Progress bar showing completion across: Onboarding → Module 1 → Module 2 → Module 3 → Module 4 → 1st Waste WAR Battle → 2nd Battle → 3rd Battle 

  **Milestone Achievements:** 

* **"Most Valuable Workplace Waste Eliminator":** Complete 4 modules \+ 1st Waste WAR Battle → Unlock affiliate revenue opportunities   
* **"Certified CI Done Right Consultant":** Complete 2nd and 3rd Waste WAR Battles → Unlock consulting opportunities 

  **5\. The CI Done Right Professor (AI Q\&A Bot)** 

  AI-powered chatbot trained on "Bulletproof Your Manager Career" book (195 pages) that answers continuous improvement questions in real-time. Provides instant guidance when users are stuck or need clarification on methodology.
