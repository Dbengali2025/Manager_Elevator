## 01 · STEP ONE — Create Your Account

Go to **app.managerelevator.com** and click **Sign up**.

**1 — Fill in your profile.** Your full name, email address, and a password (at least 8 characters, with 1 uppercase letter and 1 number). Then your HBCU alma mater (choose "None" if that's not you), company, industry, and current role.

**2 — Check your email for a 6-digit verification code.** Enter it on the screen that appears. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a couple of minutes — and if you still don't see it, click **"Didn't get a code? Resend it."**

**3 — You're verified.** Next stop: choosing your membership.

> 💡 Tip: use an email you check regularly — your weekly progress digest and milestone celebrations will be sent there. You can adjust what you receive anytime in Settings.

## 05 · STEP FIVE — Work the Masterclass

1. Open **Masterclass** and start with Module 1, Lesson 1.
2. Watch each lesson, then **mark it complete** — your progress bar and unlocks depend on it.
3. **Download the lesson tools** attached to each lesson (templates, worksheets, and slides you'll use at work).
4. Pass the module quiz at the end of each module to lock in what you learned.

> 💡 Stuck on a concept? Ask CI Professor to explain it a different way — it knows every lesson and every chapter of the book.

---

## 06 · STEP SIX — Track Your Wins

This is what separates Manager Elevator from a course you watch and forget:

1. **Log an Improvement Opportunity** — a waste, delay, or frustration you've spotted at work.
2. **Capture the Winning Solution** — what you changed, using what you learned.
3. **Record the numbers** — what the metric was before, and what it is after. Manager Elevator computes your ROI: a 40-minute process cut to 25 minutes is a 37.5% improvement.
4. **Watch your Success Dashboard grow** — milestones, nuggets, and a Monday email digest of your momentum.

These are the receipts for your next promotion conversation.

## Key Terms Explained

| | |
|:-:|:-:|
| **Continuous Improvement (CI)** An approach where small, measurable improvements are made all the time — not one big fix once a year. | **Improvement Opportunity** Any waste, delay, or frustration you spot that could be reduced or removed. |
| **Waste WAR Battle** A focused session where you and your team attack a specific waste together — Dana's signature method for making improvement a team sport. | **Winning Solution** The change you made that fixed an improvement opportunity — captured so the win is documented and repeatable. |
| **ROI (Return on Improvement)** The measurable impact of your change: ((Before − After) ÷ Before) × 100 for anything you reduced. | **Success Nugget** A short, AI-written summary of one of your wins — ready to share with your leadership. |
| **Milestone** A progress marker you hit on the platform — lessons finished, modules completed, results logged. | **Manager Value Survey** A recurring self-assessment that shows how your effectiveness is trending over time. |
